Market Insights

Demand and Supply are fundamental concepts in economics. They explain how the market operates, with demand representing the quantity of a product that consumers are willing to purchase, and supply representing the quantity that producers are willing to provide. The balance between these forces determines prices in a market.

Frequently Asked Questions

Supply and demand are fundamental economic concepts that describe the relationship between the availability of a resource and the desire for that resource.

Prices are determined by supply and demand because when demand exceeds supply, prices tend to rise. When supply exceeds demand, prices tend to fall.

Demand drives the production of goods and services. When demand for a product increases, producers tend to make more of it, which can stimulate economic growth.
